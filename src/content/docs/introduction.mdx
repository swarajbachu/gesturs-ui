# Gesturs UI

Hey there! 👋 Welcome to Gesturs UI - my little playground of cool components that I've been building for fun. Think of it as your friendly neighborhood component library where you can just grab whatever catches your eye and drop it into your projects. I mainly focus on making stuff that'll make your landing pages look awesome!

## Philosophy

**Look, here's the deal** - I absolutely love tinkering with design and creating cool stuff! That's basically why Gesturs UI exists. I'm building these components using [shadcn](https://ui.shadcn.com) (because it's awesome), and thought "hey, why not share these with everyone?"

Here's what I'm all about:

- **Keep it Simple** - No complicated stuff. Just copy, paste, and you're good to go!
- **Make it Pretty** - Because who doesn't love things that look nice? 🎨
- **Everyone's Welcome** - Built with accessibility in mind, because the web should be for everyone
- **Actually Useful** - No fancy-but-useless stuff here. Everything's battle-tested and ready to rock

The coolest part? You don't need to install a bunch of extra packages or deal with complex setups. Just grab what you like and boom - you're ready to roll! Think of it as borrowing ingredients from a friend's kitchen - take what you need, use it how you want!

## Features

Everything here is built on top of shadcn components (because why reinvent the wheel, right?). Each component is like a little LEGO piece - well-tested, looks great, and plays nice with others. Just plug and play!

Gesturs UI components are built using shadcn components, making them super accessible and scalable. Each component is carefully designed and tested to ensure it works seamlessly in your projects.
